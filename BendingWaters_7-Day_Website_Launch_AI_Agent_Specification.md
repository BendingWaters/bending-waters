**Purpose:** This document is the single source of truth for an AI development agent responsible for designing and building the BendingWaters 7-Day Website Launch landing page and its supporting conversion funnel.

---

## 1. Project Definition

Build a premium, high-converting landing page and lead-generation funnel for BendingWaters.

The primary product is:

> ***The 7-Day Website Launch Programme***

This is a productised website service designed for established founders, growing businesses, startups, SMEs and business owners who need a credible, professional digital presence before pursuing important opportunities.

These opportunities may include:

-   Investor conversations

-   Grant applications

-   Corporate partnerships

-   Business expansion

-   Customer acquisition

-   Strategic partnerships

-   Institutional opportunities

-   General business growth

The website must NOT feel like a generic freelance web-design landing page.

It should position BendingWaters as a strategic digital partner helping serious businesses become digitally ready for serious opportunities.

## 4. Primary Business Objectives

The landing page has two primary conversion objectives.

# Conversion A --- Qualified Lead

Capture:

-   First name

-   Business email

-   WhatsApp number

-   Business name

-   Business stage

-   Current website situation

-   Opportunity type

-   Project timeline

-   Preferred next step

Then allow the visitor to:

> ***Book a Strategy Call***

or continue to nurturing/sales follow-up.

# Conversion B --- Direct Purchase

A qualified visitor should be able to:

1.  Select a package.

2\. Review its scope.

3\. Provide contact details.

4\. Accept the project terms.

5\. Proceed to Paystack.

6\. Complete payment.

7\. Return to the application.

8\. Begin onboarding.

The application must support both high-consideration buyers and ready-to-buy customers.

## 28. Payment Architecture

Use Paystack as the payment processor.

The payment architecture should follow:

User

↓

Select Package

↓

Capture Lead

↓

Create Payment

↓

Paystack Checkout

↓

Paystack Webhook

↓

Server-side Verification

↓

Confirm Transaction

↓

Update Database

↓

Mark Lead as PAID

↓

Create Onboarding State

↓

Send Confirmation

↓

Onboarding

Do NOT treat the frontend redirect alone as proof of payment.

The backend must verify the transaction.

Never expose secret payment credentials in frontend code.
